# Acme Innovations - Employee Handbook & Guidelines

## 1. Work Schedule & Flexibility
- **Core Hours**: 07:00 AM to 4:00 PM in your local time zone.
- **Remote Work**: Acme Innovations operates with a All type model. Employees are welcome to work 100% remotely or from designated regional hubs.
- **Async Communication**: We prioritize written, asynchronous updates over synchronous meetings whenever possible.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave
- **Vacation**: All full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year.
- **Sick Leave**: Unlimited paid sick days for physical or mental health recovery.
- **Parental Leave**: 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary and secondary caregivers.
- **Sabbatical**: After 4 continuous years of service, employees qualify for a 4-week fully paid sabbatical.

## 3. Benefits & Learning Stipend
- **Health Coverage**: Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance paid 100% by Acme Innovations.
- **Learning & Development**: An annual budget of $5000 per employee for books, conferences, certifications, and courses.
- **Home Office Allowance**: A one-time $2000 setup reimbursement for desks, chairs, and monitors.

## 4. Security & Data Protection
- Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is mandatory on all company accounts.
- Company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker / FileVault).
- Never share production credentials or customer PII via unencrypted channels.
