# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How do I submit an expense report?
A: Submit all expense receipts through the internal portal (ExpenseFlow) by the 25th of each month. Approvals take 2-3 business days and reimbursements are processed with the subsequent payroll.

### Q: What is the policy on contributing to Open Source projects?
A: Acme encourages open-source contributions! If the project is unrelated to core proprietary IP, no pre-clearance is required. For company-sponsored repositories, submit an internal RFC before public release.

### Q: How do I request access to NovaCloud production environments?
A: File a JIRA ticket under the `INFRA-ACCESS` queue with business justification. Production access requires approval from your Engineering Director and the Security Team, followed by temporary credential issuance via Teleport.

### Q: Can I use personal laptops for company work?
A: No. All company work involving source code, customer data, or internal systems must be performed on Acme-provisioned managed laptops for compliance reasons.
